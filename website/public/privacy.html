Information handled

Pick AI History handles your account identifier and email through Firebase Authentication. It stores the questions, answers, timestamps, and any screenshots you choose to save in the app so your signed-in account can view its own history.

Service providers

The web app uses Firebase Authentication, Cloudflare Workers and D1, and Backblaze B2 object storage. Those providers process data only to operate the service. Do not upload information you do not want stored in your history.
